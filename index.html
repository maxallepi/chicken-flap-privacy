Chicken Flap — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app “Chicken Flap” (package com.chickenflap.game)
Short version: Chicken Flap collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. Your scores stay on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it works the way you expect:

  • Your best scores and local leaderboard entries
  • Unlocked skins, coins, and in-game progress
  • Sound and display preferences

This data is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Network access

The released version of Chicken Flap does not request the Android internet permission. It cannot send or receive data over a network.

Advertising and purchases

The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. No advertising or analytics SDKs are included.

Children’s privacy

Chicken Flap is a simple arcade game suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Third parties

No third-party services receive data from this app. Google Play itself may collect install and crash information as part of the Android platform and the Play Store; that is covered by Google’s privacy policy, not this one.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/maxallepi/chicken-flap-privacy/issues.

Chicken Flap is an independent game. This page is the official privacy policy referenced by its Google Play listing.
